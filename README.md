# AdMOS Technical Articles

This repository hosts the source files for the **AdMOS Technical Articles** published at **[articles.admos.de](https://articles.admos.de)**.

The Technical Articles series provides accessible, application-oriented insights into semiconductor device physics, electrical characterization, reliability, low-frequency noise, and device-to-system modeling and simulation.

## Article Series

The articles are organized into dedicated series, each focusing on a specific area of semiconductor technology.

* **LFN — Low-Frequency Noise**
  Physical origins, interpretation, and analysis of low-frequency noise and random telegraph noise in electronic devices.

* **MAC — Measurement & Characterization**
  Practical approaches to electrical measurements, characterization techniques, measurement setups, and data analysis.

* **RVA — Reliability & Variability**
  Device degradation, defect dynamics, variability, reliability mechanisms, and their growing importance in nanoscale technologies.

* **MOD — Modeling & Simulation**
  Modeling and simulation across different abstraction levels, from semiconductor devices to circuits and systems.

Additional series and articles will be added as the collection grows.

## About the Articles

The articles are written to bridge the gap between fundamental device physics and practical engineering. Each article focuses on a specific topic and combines concise technical explanations with illustrations, measurement examples, and physical interpretation.

The goal is not only to describe **what** is observed, but also to provide an intuitive understanding of **why** it happens and **why it matters**.
